01 / The idea

So, what is an AI gender swap?

An AI gender swap is a generated variation of an existing photograph. Rather than simply placing a preset filter over a face, a generative image model can reinterpret hair, facial styling, and other visual cues to create a more masculine- or feminine-presenting portrait.

Tools such as Gendora’s AI Gender Swap Filter ↗ accept a portrait, let you choose the transformation direction, and produce a new still image to download. The goal is a recognizable creative reinterpretation—not a factual prediction of someone’s appearance.

“The interesting part isn’t becoming someone else. It’s seeing something familiar from a new angle.”

04 / Common questions

Good to know.

Can I try the AI gender swap filter for free?

Yes. Gendora offers a limited free guest trial. Once the trial is used, additional edits may require an account or a paid plan. See the current pricing page for up-to-date details.

Do I need to install an application?

No. Gendora runs through a web browser on supported devices. This page is a guide; to create images, use the actual online editor.

Will the generated portrait look exactly like the original person?

Not necessarily. The model aims to preserve recognizable features, but it may alter some details in the face, clothing, or background. Results also depend on lighting, pose, and image quality.

Can I edit a group photo?

The editor is intended for one adult per image and does not offer a person selector. For a group photo, crop a separate image of each consenting adult before editing.

What about photo privacy?

Gendora’s published policy describes a 7-day retention period for uploaded images and says uploaded photos are not used to train a model. Photos are processed through storage and AI service providers. Review the privacy policy before uploading sensitive images, and always get permission from the person pictured.

Can I use an AI-edited portrait commercially?

Your rights depend on the original image, consent and likeness rights, and relevant terms. Making an AI edit does not give you permission to use someone else’s image or identity without authorization.
